Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026 · O'Escape

This Privacy Policy explains how O'Escape (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use the mobile application.

1. Information we collect

The App is designed to collect as little personal data as possible.

  • Gameplay data stored on your device: best scores, sound/mute preference, and flash-reduction preference. This data stays on your device unless you choose to share a score through your device’s share sheet.
  • Advertising identifiers: if ads are shown, advertising partners may use device advertising IDs and similar identifiers as described in their policies.
  • Diagnostics: your device or app store may collect crash or performance data according to your OS and store settings. We do not operate a separate analytics account for the App beyond what the platform or ad SDK may provide.

2. How we use information

  • To save your progress preferences and high scores on-device.
  • To show advertisements (when enabled) and measure ad performance via our advertising partners.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.

3. Advertising

The App may display ads served by Google AdMob and related Google advertising services. These partners may collect and process data (such as advertising ID, IP address, and device information) to provide and personalize ads, subject to their own privacy policies.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can often limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example, “Opt out of Ads Personalization” / “Delete advertising ID” on Android).

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers solely to run the App and ads (for example, Google). If you use the share feature, the content you choose to share is sent through the apps you select on your device.

5. Data retention

On-device preferences and scores remain until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can help address it.

7. Your choices

  • Mute sound and reduce flashes in the App settings.
  • Clear App storage or uninstall to remove local scores and preferences.
  • Adjust advertising preferences in your device settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about O'Escape, contact the developer through the app listing on Google Play or the support channel listed there.
