Privacy Policy

O'Sort · Last updated: 11 August 2026

O'Sort (“the App”) is a free puzzle game published under the package name com.app.osort. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

Information stored on your device

Gameplay progress and preferences are saved only on your device (for example via Android DataStore). This includes items such as:

  • Current level and unlock progress
  • Best move counts for levels and Daily Lab
  • Sound, haptics, and color-code settings
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial

The App does not operate user accounts and does not upload this progress to our own servers.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ad types may include banners, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads (for example, an optional extra tube).

Google and its partners may collect and process information such as device identifiers (including advertising ID where available), IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is governed by Google’s policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy guidance.

You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings (for example, Android Settings → Google → Ads, or equivalent privacy controls on your device).

Analytics and accounts

The App does not include a first-party analytics SDK, crash-reporting service, or account / sign-in system operated by us.

Internet use

An internet connection is used for advertising (and may be used by Google Play services features such as downloadable fonts). Core puzzle gameplay works with progress stored locally.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own systems. Advertising partners may process data as described above under their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when that happens.

Contact

For privacy questions about O'Sort, contact the developer using the email address listed on the Google Play store listing for this App.
