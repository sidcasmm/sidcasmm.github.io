Privacy Policy Ludo Vibe

Effective 30 October 2026. This policy covers the Ludo Vibe Android app.

Ludo Vibe is a board game you can play alone, on one phone with friends, or online. This page explains what the app stores, what other players can see, and what it does not collect.

Who this is for

Ludo Vibe is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Information stored on your phone

Most of the game lives on the device. That includes:

  • A guest name, created on the phone, such as Royale followed by a number.
  • Your coin balance, dice you own, sound setting, and daily fortune-wheel time.
  • Wins, losses, and a history of games played on that phone.
  • A friend code, if you use friends.
  • If you sign in, a copy of your display name, email address, and profile photo address, so the app can show them again after you close it.

Android backup or a phone-to-phone transfer may include this on-device data, because the app allows backup. Uninstalling the app removes it from that phone.

Account sign-in

You can play as a guest. A guest is given an anonymous account so online play and friends can work. That account is an identifier. It is not your real name.

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares with the app the name, email address, and profile photo on that Google account. We use them to show who you are on your profile and, if you play online, at the table. Signing in is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication. Their handling of that sign-in is also covered by Google’s privacy policy.

The app includes the Facebook login library, with automatic event logging and advertising-ID collection turned off. The game does not currently offer a Facebook sign-in button.

Online play and friends

When you play online or with friends, the game uses Firebase Realtime Database so the table stays in sync. Other people in that game can see:

  • Your display name. For a guest, that is the guest name. For a Google account, that is the Google name, or a name you set in the profile.
  • Your profile photo, if you signed in with Google and the account has one.
  • Your seat, dice, moves, and whether you are still in the room.
  • Short chat messages and emotes you send during that game. Those are delivered to the players at the table. The game does not keep a lasting chat history.

Friends features also store your friend code, your name, friend requests, and a simple online presence flag, so someone you added can see that you are available and can invite you to a room. An invite includes your name and the room code.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not show ads, and the app does not use the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not take real-money payments in the app. Coins are a score inside the game, kept on the phone. They are not cash and cannot be withdrawn.

Why we use this information

  • To run the game, save your progress, and show your name and photo.
  • To match you into a room, keep the board in sync, and let friends invite you.
  • To tell your account apart from a guest on the same phone, including wins, losses, and the starting coin gift.

Who else receives it

Other players in your room see the name, photo, and moves described above. We do not give your email address to other players.

Google provides sign-in and the Firebase services that host accounts, rooms, and friend data. That processing can happen outside the country where you live. We do not share your information with other companies for their own marketing.

How long it is kept

On-device data stays until you clear the app’s storage, uninstall the app, or sign out of a Google account on that phone. Signing out removes the saved Google name, email, and photo from the app on that phone and shows the guest profile again.

Online room data is kept while the room exists so the game can continue. Presence is removed when you leave. If you want the online account and related room or friend records deleted, email us at the address below. We will delete what we can still identify as yours.

Security

Online data is sent over an encrypted connection and is stored in Firebase, which only signed-in players can access. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use Ludo Vibe after that date means you accept the updated policy.
